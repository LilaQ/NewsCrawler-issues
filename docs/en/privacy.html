Pre-release notice. The final operator/controller legal identity and postal contact must be approved before App Store submission. This page accurately describes the current technical data flows but is not a substitute for that owner decision.

NewsBar is designed without an owner-operated account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking SDK or content-proxy backend.

Data stored on the Mac

Preferences, enabled and custom feed sources, custom categories, topic follow/mute rules, notification deduplication identifiers, local recommendation counters, read state and bookmarks remain in local app storage. The optional menu-bar marquee stores at most ten headline strings per language so it can resume after relaunch. It does not store article text, URLs, images or summaries for this purpose.

Direct publisher requests

NewsBar contacts configured publishers, the official Hacker News API and image CDNs directly to retrieve feeds, article pages and images. These providers receive ordinary technical connection data such as the device IP address and apply their own privacy policies. Preview images are downsampled and kept only in a bounded memory cache. Article pages are processed locally in an invisible, non-persistent WebKit session with publisher JavaScript disabled, then rendered in NewsBar’s native reader.

Notifications and on-device processing

Optional local notifications can show requested topic matches or a Morning Brief reminder. NewsBar does not register for remote push notifications or upload a device token. The German presentation option rewrites visible German text locally. On supported macOS 26 devices, a user may explicitly ask Apple’s on-device Foundation Model to compare up to eight visible cluster headlines and feed summaries. NewsBar does not operate an AI backend or train a model.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchase information. NewsBar reads verified StoreKit entitlements to unlock Premium and sends no purchase data to an owner-operated backend.

Your choices

You can disable or remove sources and categories, remove topic rules and bookmarks, reset local recommendations, revoke notification permission in System Settings and delete the app’s local container. NewsBar has no account requiring an online deletion request.

Contact

Use the public support forms for non-sensitive privacy questions. Do not post personal data. Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting for security-sensitive matters.
